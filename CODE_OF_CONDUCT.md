# Code of conduct

Rulehall is a small project, and I'd like it to stay a friendly place.

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.

If someone makes you feel unwelcome, tell me in private through the [private report form](https://github.com/mmerah/rulehall/security/advisories/new). It isn't only for security issues. I'll read every report and act on it, which can mean a warning, a removed comment or a ban from the project.
