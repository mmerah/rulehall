# 5e Lite

A cut of the fifth edition rules from the System Reference Document 5.2.1 (the 2024 rules): one hero and up to two hired companions, levels 1 to 5, and turn-based battles on a battle screen. Outside a battle the master tells the story and names words, not numbers, to its tools. Inside a battle the player plays the party, the opponent role plays the foes, and code resolves every action.

## Official sources

- **SRD 5.2.1**, the official PDF: <https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf>, linked from <https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd>. It has 364 pages, and the PDF page numbers match the printed ones. The PDF prints no date; its metadata says it was made on 2025-04-23. Read 2026-10-12.
- **Structured data:** the 5e-bits database, 2024 edition, at <https://github.com/5e-bits/5e-database>. The repository is archived; its data now lives in 5e-bits/5e-srd-api. It gives the numbers of monsters, spells, classes, species, backgrounds and equipment. Effects, reach, areas and most spell numbers are prose there.
- **Open5e is not used.** Its 2024 creature data (<https://api.open5e.com/v2/creatures/?document__key=srd-2024>, read 2026-10-12) has wrong damage types, sizes and XP.

This file holds no rules text. Build from the SRD, not from a summary of it.

## Pack sources

One pack ships, `src/rulehall/engines/lite5e/packs/srd.json`. Its prose is ours. It holds a frontier backdrop (the Marchlands: small walled towns, old roads, ruins and wild land), names, locations and seeds, people and monsters that each name a stat block of the bestiary, and every background: the SRD four and twelve of ours (Archivist, Drover, Envoy, Fisher, Forgehand, Herbalist, Minstrel, Outrider, Peddler, Pilgrim, Steeplejack and Trapper). Our backgrounds have the SRD's shape: three abilities, one origin feat of the SRD four, two skills, one tool, and one gear list of SRD items at SRD prices.

A written pack can do the same. It can also rename a stat block (SRD 5.2.1 has no orc stat block, so a pack may list an "orc raider" with the Tough stat block: the name is the pack's, the numbers are the SRD's). The game has no firearms: the export leaves out the musket, the pistol and their bullets.

## The scope

- Levels 1 to 5, at 300, 900, 2,700 and 6,500 XP.
- The twelve SRD classes, each with its one SRD subclass from level 3. Twelve shipped characters, one per class.
- The nine SRD species with their lineages, ancestries and legacies.
- The SRD four backgrounds and our twelve, the SRD's origin and Fighting Style feats, and Ability Score Improvement.
- The spells of levels 0 to 3 on the eight class spell lists.
- The SRD gear, and these magic items: Potions of Healing, Weapons +1 and +2, Armor +1 and Shield +1.
- The bestiary: the SRD stat blocks of CR 0 to 5 that code can play.

## The tools

The master's tools, in words: `check` (an ability check), `harm` (a trap, a save against a danger, certain harm or a fall), `cast` (a spell outside a battle), `rest`, `reward` (Heroic Inspiration, the XP of a goal, or a change of the party's coins), `make_item` (a story item), `trade` (buy or sell at a shop), `start_battle`, `kill` (a captive), `join_party` (a hire or a follower) and `leave_party`. The map tools that every map engine has stay: move, reveal, unlock a way, `move_item` (which also takes loot), meanwhile, extend the map and direct. The docstrings of the tools are exactly what the model reads.

## Player actions

The master never sees these. The player takes them on the panels, in the decisions and on the battle screen:

- **Before a battle:** set each member's row.
- **Gear:** equip and unequip, drink a potion, give an item to a member, drop an item here.
- **Lay On Hands** and **Font of Magic**, outside a battle, on the Party panel.
- **After a short rest:** spend Hit Dice. **After a long rest:** change the prepared spells.
- **The level-up picks.** A pending level-up swap of a prepared spell stays open across a long rest until it is made.
- **In a battle:** on a member's own turn, its actions and their variants, a demand, or the party's yield; at any time, Auto for a companion. During a battle, the panels refuse every edit.

## Deviations in this repo

Every divergence between the code and SRD 5.2.1, with the reason it stands. A rule not listed here is played as the SRD prints it.

### The battle's shape

1. **Rows and range bands, not a grid.** Each side has a front row and a back row. Three bands stand in for distance: close (your own row, or front row to enemy front row), near and far (back row to enemy back row). Melee reaches close, a range of 30 ft or less reaches near, a longer range reaches far, and long range is ignored; a Reach weapon also reaches the enemy front row from your back row. When a side's front row has no active creature, its back row counts as its front row for reach and attacks. Dash and Hide still need you in your own back row, and Withdraw your own front row. This is because a model cannot reason well about a grid, and rows keep the choices that matter: melee or range, engaged or not, who is exposed.
2. **Areas hit picked foes in one row, and never allies.** A 5-ft radius takes up to 2 foes. A Cone or Cube of 15 ft or less, a 10-ft radius or any Line takes up to 4. A larger area takes every standing foe in the row. This is because rows cannot show where the allies stand inside an area.
3. **Speed counts only as 0 or more.** A turn has one row change. Dash only leaves the battle, from your back row; Step of the Wind paid with a Focus Point leaves it from either row. Foes do not pursue. Speed above 30 ft, climbing, flying and every other movement mode have no effect. This is because the rows have no feet to walk.
4. **One opportunity attack, made by code.** A creature that moves from its front row to its back row while the enemy front row has an active creature takes one melee attack from the engaged enemy with the best melee bonus. It spends no reaction, so the same enemy may strike on each such move. Disengage, Misty Step, Cloud's Jaunt, Withdraw, a push, and foes with Flyby or Agile provoke none. A push also ends the grapples on the pushed creature. This is because rows do not say who stands next to whom.
5. **No other reactions.** Counterspell, Feather Fall, Hellish Rebuke, Slow Fall, Parry and every other monster reaction are cut. Shield is Ward: a bonus action on your own turn for +5 AC until your next turn. Uncanny Dodge, Deflect Attacks (with no strike back), Stone's Endurance and Storm's Thunder act on their own when they apply. Each one takes the Reaction, so a member uses only the first that applies each round. This is because a question during a foe's turn would stop the battle.
6. **Variants, not offers.** A smite, Stunning Strike, Cunning Strike, Open Hand Technique, Eldritch Smite and Reckless Attack are menu entries picked before the roll. A cost is spent only on the first hit of that action, and a smite also spends the bonus action. Metamagic is an entry of the spell too, one option per cast: Empowered joins no other option, and Heightened acts on the first named target. Empowered rerolls each damage die that shows 1 or 2, up to its cap (the Charisma modifier, at least 1), with no pick. This is because a question after every hit slows each turn.
7. **Automatic spends.** The Halfling's Luck, then Bardic Inspiration, then Heroic Inspiration act on their own on a failed d20 Test. The Bardic die is spent only when it turns the failure into a success; otherwise the holder keeps it until the battle ends. Tactical Mind acts on its own, outside a battle only. This is because a choice after every roll slows the game for a small gain.
8. **Hide and light are coarse.** Hide needs cover at the place and you in your back row, and is a Stealth check against DC 15. You stay Invisible until you attack or cast a spell with a Verbal component. Cover gives no AC. A light source that a member carries raises the place's light one step, for both sides of the battle. Darkness Blinds a creature with no Darkvision against what it cannot see. This is because rows cannot say what stands between two creatures, or how far a light reaches.
9. **Initiative by group.** Each foe group makes one Initiative roll with its Dexterity modifier, not the stat block's Initiative bonus. A tie goes to the higher Dexterity, then to the party. This is because one roll per group keeps the order short, and the Dexterity modifier is the SRD's base rule.
10. **Foes are planned, not judged.** Each turn code ranks a foe's entries: a ready entry with a recharge or a daily use first, then expected damage times the targets it reaches. A foe starts in the front row when an entry with its best expected damage is a melee entry, and in the back row otherwise. Each foe rolls a d10 for instinct against a score from its Intelligence (8, 6, 4 or 2); on instinct it takes its first legal entry against the last creature that hurt it, or the nearest. The opponent role picks the plans of the others from the legal list, or code takes the first legal entry when the role is off. This is because the SRD leaves tactics to the GM, and a model that plays every foe perfectly is no fun.
11. **Morale and surrender.** A foe group makes a DC 10 Wisdom save when its first foe drops and when it falls below half its total HP, with Advantage while a standing foe of higher CR is in the battle. On a failure a Beast flees and any other foe surrenders as a captive. These groups never check: a group of one, a group whose CR is above every other foe's, and Constructs, Plants and Undead. A demand is Intimidation against the higher of 15 and the group's Intelligence, once per group in a battle. It is legal only once a foe of the group dropped or the group is below half its total HP, and a success forces a check. Fled foes form a routed group. When the party fights it again, it checks with Disadvantage before the first turn, and on a failure it surrenders, Beasts too. An Intimidation success against a routed group outside a battle makes it a captive group. A routed group regroups at the party's next long rest. A captive or routed group holds 8 at most; the rest scatter, and the story is told so. This is because the SRD has no morale rule, and a battle should be able to end before the last foe drops.
12. **The battle's end.** When the hero dies, the battle ends at once, and the game ends. Otherwise the battle ends when no foe stands, when the hero left and no member who stayed stands, or when no member who stayed is active; the player can also yield. After a defeat, every living person at the place who did not flee stops being hostile and holds the party; the master plays capture, robbery or being left for dead. Every member at 0 HP who is not dead then stands at 1 HP, and outside a battle HP never drops below 1. This is because every death save then stays on the battle screen, where code runs it, and no number kills a member outside a battle.
13. **Durations in tiers.** One minute or less ends with the battle. Up to an hour ends when the party leaves the place or after the next battle. Eight hours or more lasts until the next long rest. Concentration ends with the battle, and a lowered HP maximum lasts until a long rest. This is because the game has no clock.

### Classes and features

14. **Levels 1 to 5, one subclass per class, no Weapon Mastery.** Above level 5, features and foes need much more code; the SRD gives one subclass per class; each mastery is a rule for one weapon kind, and several need positions.
15. **Fixed HP on a level up, option A gear at creation.** HP rises by the class's fixed value. The gold option is cut. Ammunition, encumbrance, attunement and a Healer's Kit's uses are not tracked. This is because each saves a decision or a ledger for a small gain.
16. **Features that need a position, a second creature or a movement mode are cut or narrowed.** Wild Companion, Faithful Steed, Wild Resurgence, Tactical Shift, Memorize Spell, Horde Breaker, Font of Inspiration's slot trade, the Rogue's Poison and the move of Remarkable Athlete are cut. Turn Undead sends a foe to its back row, ends its grapples and stops its turns until it takes damage; it does not flee the battle. Primal Knowledge's Strength checks while raging are cut, as Rage plays only in a battle. This is because rows have no room to flee into and no one stands beside another.
17. **Lighter readings of class features.** Rage lasts the whole battle with no upkeep, and its damage goes on Strength melee weapon attacks only. Divine Spark deals Radiant damage only. Lay On Hands heals as much of the missing HP as the pool can pay. Preserve Life splits its HP among allies at half HP or less, those at 0 HP first. Fast Hands only uses a Healer's Kit as a bonus action. Sacred Weapon is spent from the menu with no action. Wild Shape gives three fixed forms (the Wolf and the Giant Badger, and the Black Bear from level 4) with the form's stat block but your own HP and mental scores. Circle of the Land gets three land types; the polar land is cut, and the tropical land has no level 5 spell. This is because each keeps one menu entry and no extra pick.
18. **Recoveries act on their own.** Arcane Recovery and Sorcerous Restoration act at the first short rest where they give something back, highest slots first. Magical Cunning acts at the end of a battle, or after a cast outside one, when a pact slot is spent. Uncanny Metabolism acts at the first Initiative roll where it helps. This is because each would otherwise be a question at a moment the player does not watch.
19. **Bardic Inspiration and Cutting Words.** Bardic Inspiration is a bonus action that gives the die to any member but the Bard, in any band; a member holds one die. Cutting Words is Jeer, a bonus action that spends a use on any foe, which subtracts the die from its next attack roll or save. This is because the SRD's Cutting Words is a reaction.
20. **Invocations from a short list.** A Warlock picks from Armor of Shadows, Eldritch Mind, Pact of the Blade, Pact of the Tome, Agonizing Blast, Devil's Sight, Fiendish Vigor, Mask of Many Faces, Misty Visions, Repelling Blast, Eldritch Smite and Thirsting Blade. Armor of Shadows sets AC 13 + Dex with no body armor, with no cast. Fiendish Vigor gives 12 temporary HP at the start of each battle. Repelling Blast pushes to the back row. Agonizing Blast and Repelling Blast apply to Eldritch Blast only. The SRD's other invocations need a familiar, a movement mode, another creature's senses or darkness that a battle does not track.
21. **Senses, speed and languages are tags.** Fast Movement, Unarmored Movement, Second-Story Work, Divine Sense, Druidic and Thieves' Cant change no number. The master sees them on the member, with Devil's Sight, Mask of Many Faces and Misty Visions, and plays them in the story. Languages are not tracked, and the languages of Deft Explorer are cut: the master plays who understands whom. This is because the rows have no feet, and a language list would change no roll.

### Species and feats

22. **Species traits that need a map are tags.** Stonecunning, Halfling Nimbleness, Naturally Stealthy, Trance, Draconic Flight and Large Form are tags the master sees and plays outside a battle. Powerful Build gives Advantage on a check to escape a grapple, and nothing else. In a battle, Large Form is a bonus action once per long rest that gives Advantage on Strength checks and size Large; Cloud's Jaunt is a row change with no opportunity attack. Darkvision is read for light, and Trance for Sleep. This is because rows have no height, no terrain and no exact distance.
23. **Size is picked by code.** A species that may be Small or Medium, the Human and the Tiefling, plays as Medium, with no step to pick it; the Gnome and the Halfling are Small. This is because the pick would change only rare size limits.
24. **Adrenaline Rush and Breath Weapon fit the turn.** Adrenaline Rush in a battle is a bonus action that gives temporary HP equal to the proficiency bonus, with no Dash. Breath Weapon takes the place of one attack of the Attack action. This is because the Dash only leaves the battle.
25. **A casting ability is picked once.** A lineage with spells, and Magic Initiate, ask for their casting ability at creation. When Magic Initiate is taken at level 4, it asks for the ability before the spells. This is because the sheet keeps one ability for those spells.
26. **Alert's Initiative Swap is cut, and the party does not grapple.** The Grapple action and the Grappler feat are cut. Foes still grapple, and a member escapes one hold at a time. This is because the swap is a question at the start of every battle, and a grapple by a member would need rules to drag a foe between rows.
27. **The Soldier takes one fixed gaming set.** The SRD lets the Soldier pick any gaming set, so the background has one gear list.

### Spells

28. **Battle spells in fixed shapes, the rest story spells or cut.** A spell plays in a battle as one attack, a save with damage or a condition, a heal, or a buff or debuff. A spell is cut when it is a reaction, needs a second creature, positions, terrain, a movement mode or illusion rules, links two creatures, runs its own turns, or fits no group. Thunderous Smite and Wrathful Smite are not in the SRD data we use. Any other spell is a story spell: code spends the slot and rolls the saves of the persons it targets, and the master plays the effect. The Light cantrip is a story spell. This is because code plays only these shapes.
29. **Readings of single spells.** Command keeps three words: Grovel, Halt and Flee. Chromatic Orb does not leap. Acid Arrow deals its later damage with the hit. Mirror Image's three duplicates each take one hit, with no roll. Sleep and Hypnotic Pattern end only with damage or Concentration. Web restrains only the creatures it catches when it is cast. Slow takes the bonus action every turn, with no spell failure. Flame Blade, like Spiritual Weapon and Call Lightning, attacks when cast and again on later turns. Beacon of Hope takes up to three named members. A creature frightened by Fear spends each of its turns fleeing to its back row, or cowering there, and makes no save to end the spell. This is because rows cannot say who stands within 5 ft, a row has no space for a web and no line of sight, and each effect stays one menu entry.
30. **The mark moves by itself.** When the target of Hunter's Mark or Hex drops, the mark moves with no action to the nearest standing foe. Hex gives no Disadvantage on ability checks. This is because a bonus action off the member's turn would be a question, and ability checks in a battle are few.
31. **Battle spells wait for the battle.** Outside a battle, of the battle spells, only the healing spells can be cast. This is because the master starts a battle instead of dealing damage in the story.

### Monsters and foes

32. **Unplayable stat blocks are left out.** Swallow, engulf, petrification, ethereal travel, shapechangers (the Imp and the Quasit stay, their Shape-Shift dropped), Legendary Resistance and legendary actions, an Ooze's Split, and other entries code cannot play leave the stat block out. This is because each needs rules of its own.
33. **Some entries are dropped.** Every Spellcasting entry is dropped, and each caster fights with its other actions. Monster reactions are dropped, and so are moves, leaps, pulls, teleports and other entries that have no use in rows. The Mummy's curse, the Otyugh's disease and the Vampire Familiar's paralysis at 0 HP are cut; Sunlight Sensitivity is a tag. This is because they would need prompts, a spell plan or tracking after the battle.
34. **Readings of single stat blocks.** The Brass wyrmling's Sleep Breath gives Incapacitated until the end of the target's next turn, with no second save. The Barbed Devil's Multiattack keeps one Claws and one Tail, and Hurl Flame is its own attack. The Giant Crocodile's Tail may hit the target its Bite holds. The Troll's Regeneration acts only while it stands: at 0 HP it drops for good. The Giant Spider's Web holds until its target escapes with a Strength (Athletics) check against DC 13; the web has no AC or HP to attack. A Fire Aura burns the creature's standing enemies in the close band, up to the cap of its Emanation. This is because a stat block holds one list of attacks and no target rule, a battle tracks no objects, and a dropped foe stays out of the battle.

### Outside the battle

35. **The DC comes from the world.** The master names a difficulty word (easy 10, medium 15, hard 20) or a target: a hidden thing, a locked way and a trap carry their own word, a hidden person may carry its Stealth total instead, and a person's DC is the higher of 15 and its Intelligence, or its passive Perception or Deception. The SRD's very easy, very hard and nearly impossible are cut. This is because a model that picks numbers picks them badly, and a check that easy needs no roll.
36. **An unseen person hides by its Stealth.** An unknown person at the party's place with no find word rolls Stealth when it walks in, when the party arrives, and at the opening: one roll per group, with its Stealth bonus or else its Dexterity modifier. The roll faces the party's best passive Perception, with the light applied. When the Perception meets or beats the total, the story tells who noticed the person, and it is known. Otherwise it stays hidden until the party or the person leaves, and its Stealth total is the DC of a Perception or Investigation check that finds it. A foe ambush uses the same total. The SRD's Hide action, with its DC 15, cover and sight lines, does not apply outside a battle. This is because a place has no positions or sight lines: one roll when a person comes into the party's place stands in for the Hide action, and a search finds a hidden person as it finds a hidden thing.
37. **Damage outside a battle comes in three sizes.** Light, moderate and heavy are 1d6, 2d6 and 3d6 at levels 1-2, and 2d6, 3d6 and 5d6 at levels 3-5. A success on the save always halves it. This is because the sizes keep a trap from killing a level 1 hero on a whim.
38. **One check per attempt.** After a failure, the same member cannot make the same check against the same target, or for the same named attempt, until the party moves or rests. This is because a failure should change the situation.
39. **Heroic Inspiration once between long rests.** The master gives Heroic Inspiration to a member at most once between long rests. This is because the SRD leaves the gift to the GM, and a model gives it too often.
40. **Rest pacing without a clock.** A long rest needs a battle or a first visit to a place since the last one, and two short rests at most come between long rests. No rest while a hostile person is here or one open way away. At a long rest, a hostile group with a path to the party breaks it on a 1 on a d6. This is because the game has no hours: a battle or a new place stands in for the 16-hour limit, and a die, not the master, decides an interruption.
41. **XP goes to the hero, and the party levels with the hero.** A battle pays the XP of the foes that dropped, surrendered or fled, split equally among the members alive at its end, rounded down; the hero gains its share. A goal pays the hero the XP budget of its size at the party's level. A group whose XP was paid, such as captives or a routed group, gives none again. Every member levels up when the hero does. This is because the SRD does not say how hired companions count, and a rout should never cost the party.
42. **The player plays the companions.** A companion is a class companion built at the party's level, or a stat-block companion of CR up to 1/4 at level 1, 1 at levels 2-3 and 2 at levels 4-5. Auto plays a companion for a player who does not want to. This is because the SRD has one player per character and no rule for monster companions, and the cap keeps one from outfighting the party.

### Also narrowed

43. **Four Metamagic options.** A Sorcerer picks from Empowered, Heightened, Quickened and Twinned Spell. Careful, Distant, Extended, Seeking, Subtle and Transmuted Spell are cut. Heightened acts on the first save the spell asks for. This is because the cut options change areas, ranges, durations, sight or components that rows and tiers do not track.
44. **Heavy and Loading do nothing.** A Small creature or a low score takes no Disadvantage with a Heavy weapon, and a Loading weapon fires as often as its attacks allow. This is because each would need its own check on every attack for little play.
45. **One Exhaustion level per place between rests.** A save outside a battle adds at most one Exhaustion level at each place until the party rests. This is because a danger the master calls again and again would stack Exhaustion with no clock.
46. **Ambushes for the whole party.** A party ambush surprises all the foes or none: every member's Stealth must meet or beat their best passive Perception, and then each member starts the battle Invisible. A foe ambush uses the lowest Stealth total among the hidden foes against each member's passive Perception. This is because a group acts as one, and rows have no lines of sight to hide in one by one.
47. **No swaps of earlier picks.** The cantrip swap on a level up, the Wizard's cantrip swap on a long rest, the invocation swap, the Fighting Style swap, Magic Initiate's Spell Change and Font of Magic's conversion of a spell slot into Sorcery Points are cut, and Magic Initiate is taken only once. This is because each is an extra pick for a small gain.

## What the AI game master adds

- **Hidden from the narrator.** No told fact holds a foe's or a person's HP, AC, CR, XP, DC or passive Perception, in a battle or outside it. The narrator sees health bands (Unhurt, Hurt, Bloodied, Down) and outcomes. The opening, the Initiative order, a check against a person, a demand and a morale check tell only their outcome.
- **Data on people and places.** The worldsmith writes a stat block and a count on each person or group (Commoner when it names none), whether it is hostile, a find word on each hidden item and on each hidden person that does not hide by its Stealth, a lock word on each locked way, a hazard on each trap, the light and the cover of each place, the shops, and the goals with their sizes.
- **Words, not numbers.** The difficulty words and the damage sizes let the master judge without numbers. The master sees each person's difficulty for the party, and each member's light and tags. Starting a battle never refuses a fight over budget.
- **Battle openings.** Face to face, a party ambush (the foes are surprised only when every member's Stealth matches or beats their best passive Perception) or a foe ambush (the lowest Stealth total of the hidden foes against each member's passive Perception; a group uses the total it rolled when it came in, or rolls one now).
- **A world that moves.** Between scenes the master moves patrols and hostile groups with meanwhile, and a hostile group can break a long rest. After a battle, fled foes and members who escaped walk one unlocked way out of the place, picked by a die.
- **Loot and story items.** Foes leave one item for each SRD weapon their attacks name. A story item has a name and a note and no rules; a story key opens the one way with a lock that it names. Time passing offscreen never opens a way with a lock.

## Where the rules live

Mechanics are in `src/rulehall/engines/lite5e/`: the sheet, the world, and the `battle/` package. The committed data in `src/rulehall/engines/lite5e/data/` is generated by the export scripts in `src/rulehall/engines/lite5e/srd/`. Never edit it by hand. The scripts merge the SRD source with the hand-written overlay in `src/rulehall/engines/lite5e/srd/overlay/`, which is the transcription of record: the class, spell, monster, battle and item tables, the overrides and the art. The overlay holds only 5e Lite decisions; the export derives everything else and refuses an overlay value it would derive anyway. The map machinery is `MapEngine` in `src/rulehall/engines/bases/maps/`. The master's instructions are in `src/rulehall/engines/lite5e/rules.md`.
