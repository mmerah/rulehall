# The Drowned Road

The causeway to Saint Ferrant runs three miles over tidal flats and is walkable
for four hours either side of low water.

> The bell rings twice. The water is already over your boots, and the chapel
> lamp is still a mile off.

The Bell House stands at the causeway's landward end. Its keeper, Ovid Sarn,
rings the tide bell twice a day.
