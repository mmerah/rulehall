The Drowned Lighthouse

A one-night adventure for a lone hero.

Premise

The keeper of Grey Point vanished in the autumn storm. The lamp still turns every night, and ships that follow it do not come home.

Places

The Causeway. A stone path that floods at high tide.

The Lamp Room. The great lens turns with no hand on the crank.

The Cistern. Salt water below the tower, and something that breathes in it.

People

Wenna Rook, the keeper's daughter, who rows out each dawn to look for him.

Secrets

The keeper made a bargain with the sea. The light is his half of it.
